About the data & sources

Compiled from Pop Mart's official regional sites, mall directories and news coverage of openings. Verified means a reliable source confirms the store opened and no closure was found. Unverified means it was announced but its opening couldn't be confirmed, or only weak or conflicting reports exist. Coordinates were placed by hand at mall or street level, so treat them as approximate. Directions links search by name and address. Pop-ups that had already closed were left out.
