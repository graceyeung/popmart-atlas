About the data & sources

Compiled from Pop Mart's official regional sites, mall directories and news coverage of openings. Most mainland China stores come from Baidu Maps listings (checked 24 November 2026), with ROBO SHOP vending machines, pop-ups and places Baidu marks as closed left out. They are marked unverified because a map listing doesn't confirm a store is still open, and Baidu lists more locations than the 419 mainland stores Pop Mart reported. Verified means a reliable source confirms the store opened and no closure was found. Unverified means it was announced but its opening couldn't be confirmed, or only weak or conflicting reports exist. Coordinates were placed by hand at mall or street level, so treat them as approximate. Directions links search by name and address. Pop-ups that had already closed were left out.
